Macau Sauna Market Update — September 2026: Yu Sauna Opens, Victoria Returns and Nine Major Venues Assessed

Macau's sauna market shifted again in September 2026 as Yu Sauna launched with a 100-plus roster and Victoria Sauna reopened under a new team. This update assesses Empire, Shang Pin, Majesty, Manhao, East Castle, Number Nine and The Excellent, covering the latest observed line-ups, promotions and venue-selection guidance.

Macau Sauna Sites · Updated Sep 1, 2026 · 10 min read

Although Macau is saying goodbye to a sweltering August and entering early autumn, the weather remains hot and humid — and the sauna market has lost none of its heat.

Within a single month spanning late August and early September, Macau’s sauna landscape shifted noticeably again. Newcomer Yu Sauna made a forceful debut and quickly disrupted what had been a relatively quiet market. A number of venues responded by adjusting promotions and adding treatment and booking benefits in an effort to retain existing customers.

By the final weekend of August, Yu Sauna’s on-site roster had passed 100. Meanwhile, long-established Victoria Sauna reopened under a new team. After the upheaval in June, Macau’s sauna market is gradually regaining momentum and entering another round of intense competition.

Prices are changing just as frequently. When the information was updated on 30 August, discounts at some venues had briefly exceeded MOP 700, although the venues also indicated that the relevant campaigns might last only three days. Prices and promotions remain in a period of concentrated adjustment, so menus, discounts and added benefits can all change at short notice.

Information date: This article brings together market observations from 30 August to 1 September 2026. Venue rosters, traffic, promotions and service arrangements can change at any time, so confirm again before setting out.

Prices and promotions in this article should therefore be treated only as a snapshot. Before visiting, always reconfirm the day’s prices, service fees, campaign rules and final amount payable. Booking in advance will generally still let you confirm a better-value plan, complimentary transfers and extra treatment arrangements, but the venue’s same-day reply takes precedence.

What Changed in Macau’s Sauna Market in September 2026?

Macau’s sauna market is no longer dominated by just two or three large venues.

Yu Sauna has quickly attracted attention with a core team from the former Oceanic Royal operation and a 100-plus roster. Empire Sauna remains in the leading group on the strength of its Chinese line-up, luxurious setting and service reputation. Majesty Spa raises the value proposition with substantial promotions and overnight capability. Shang Pin Spa and Manhao Spa continue to compete for Taipa traffic, while East Castle Spa retains its distinctiveness through scripted theme rooms.

Several features now define the market:

  • The 100-plus large-venue model has returned with Yu Sauna’s opening
  • Venues are increasing promotions and complimentary treatment benefits
  • Victoria has resumed operations, with established venues gradually returning
  • Therapists are moving between venues more quickly, creating marked fluctuations in individual rosters
  • There is still no undisputed, stable market leader
  • Price, service, location and distinctive experiences continue to matter more
  • Queues and room waits are returning at popular venues on weekends

Choosing a venue now means looking beyond the headcount on a single day or the lowest number on a menu. Rosters, traffic and promotions are changing quickly; observations across several points in time give a more accurate picture.

Yu Sauna: A 100-Plus Newcomer Makes a Forceful Entry

In late August 2026, Macau’s sauna market welcomed its most consequential new entrant in some time: Yu Sauna.

Some in the market have described it as a hybrid of Number One Sauna and Oceanic Royal Spa. Based on the current staff mix, the core team broadly carries over from the former Oceanic Royal operation, with some managers familiar with the Number One model also joining.

Yu Sauna only formally opened in late August, so the observation window remains short. Even so, its numerical advantage is already unmistakable. The weekend roster passed 100, far ahead of the rest of Macau’s current market and bringing back a scale not seen for some time.

What Does Yu Sauna’s Current Roster Look Like?

Yu Sauna’s main characteristics at this stage are:

  • A large number of Chinese therapists
  • A substantial Southeast Asian roster
  • A comparatively notable Japanese and Korean presence for now
  • A Caucasian roster of some scale, although not yet at Shang Pin’s peak level
  • An overall on-site headcount that has reached the 100-plus range

Numerical leadership does not, however, mean that overall quality has returned to the peak-era standards of Number One Sauna or Oceanic Royal Spa.

Early observations suggest that Yu Sauna’s average quality has not yet opened a particularly clear gap over other popular venues operating at the same time. That is not surprising: a newly opened venue needs time for its people, management and service processes to settle. Its stability after September will be more revealing.

Yu Sauna’s Setting and Rooms

Yu Sauna uses the premises of its predecessor. By current Macau standards, the setting sits roughly in the mid-range to upper-mid tier.

It is not the market’s most lavishly decorated venue, but compared with rooms at Number One Sauna and Oceanic Royal Spa in their later years, it still feels somewhat fresher and more comfortable. Bathtub rooms that previously leaned toward a Thai-bath style are gradually being converted into large waterbed configurations similar to Oceanic Royal’s, which better match the preferences of traditional Macau sauna regulars.

Dining has not yet fully carried over Oceanic Royal’s former advantage. Both the range and execution leave room for improvement, and it remains worth watching whether they are strengthened later.

What Should You Watch at Yu Sauna Right Now?

Traffic has risen quickly since the opening, and weekend queues for rooms have already begun to appear.

Some recent guests have also reported sessions occasionally running short during busy periods, at a rate that is currently higher than expected. Problems of this kind are often related to a new team settling in, sudden traffic growth and on-site management. Whether they improve will depend on the management team’s control of processes and staffing.

Overall, Yu Sauna still deserves a place on the September shortlist. But anyone arriving with the standards of Number One Sauna or Oceanic Royal Spa at their peaks may feel that some distance remains for now.

September position: Macau’s largest new venue by headcount, with a clear lead in roster scale, but quality and service consistency are still settling in.

Empire Sauna: Strong Setting, Chinese Roster and Service

Empire performed very well in mid-August. Its on-site roster briefly passed 80, while guest traffic continued to rise. The venue’s headline Chinese contingent did not disappoint: its numbers, appearance and service reputation all remained toward the upper end of Macau’s current market.

Empire’s headcount did not remain at its peak. By late August, the on-site roster had fallen back to around 40 to 50.

This may simply reflect temporary scheduling changes. The venue may also be unable to accommodate such a large therapist roster over the long term, and the possibility that some staff were drawn away by new openings cannot be ruled out. In practical terms, a roster of 40 to 50 may be more sensible: it provides enough choice without creating prolonged room waits under excessive traffic.

Empire Sauna’s Biggest Advantages

Empire still has several advantages that are difficult to overlook:

  • Particularly strong numbers and overall quality among Chinese therapists
  • Broadly complete multinational coverage
  • Well-executed room and waterbed facilities
  • Luxurious venue decor
  • Suitable for both private visits and business hosting
  • Generally stable service processes

Empire’s space, rooms and shared facilities remain in Macau’s leading tier. Whether visiting privately or arranging a more presentable venue for clients, the overall setting is unlikely to let the occasion down.

Its current 40-to-50-person scale is smaller than Yu Sauna’s, but may actually be more comfortable for guests who do not want to queue or sift through a 100-person line-up.

September position: Chinese therapists and an upscale setting remain its core strengths; after the roster contracted, choice and traffic are now better balanced.

Shang Pin Spa: Down from Its Peak, With Room for a Better Experience

Shang Pin was one of the Macau sauna market’s biggest beneficiaries in June and July, when traffic, therapist numbers and market attention all rose rapidly.

By late August, however, its traffic, on-site headcount and overall quality had all eased back. These are observations from particular points in time and do not establish that Shang Pin’s overall standard has fallen sharply. Even so, the simultaneous decline in both therapist and guest numbers is a comparatively measurable change.

Shang Pin’s biggest earlier problem was not a lack of people. It was that management and service processes failed to keep pace when too many guests arrived at once.

Common issues included:

  • Longer evening queues
  • An imbalance between therapists and guests
  • Popular choices being taken very quickly
  • Fluctuations in on-site service quality
  • Management responding more slowly than traffic was growing

With guest and therapist numbers returning to a more balanced level, the overall experience may actually have room to improve.

What Has Shang Pin Changed?

Shang Pin has recently increased its promotional offers and added more add-on massage and lobby-treatment benefits. Its signature bubble bath remains available and is still the clearest distinction between Shang Pin and other venues.

Shang Pin continues to offer several advantages:

  • A Cotai location convenient for travellers staying in the newer resort district
  • A clearly defined bubble-bath feature
  • A comparatively complete multinational roster
  • Coverage across Caucasian, Japanese/Korean, Southeast Asian and Chinese therapists
  • Stronger promotions once again
  • The prospect of less queue pressure as traffic falls back

Whether Shang Pin can reclaim a secure position as Taipa’s leading venue will depend on management, service consistency and control of peak-hour traffic.

September position: the roster and traffic have eased from their peaks, but the bubble bath, promotions and multinational coverage remain competitive.

Majesty Spa: Still the Hardest Value Proposition to Beat

When discussing value in Macau’s September sauna market, Majesty remains almost impossible to overlook.

At the time this article was updated, Majesty’s daytime campaign had briefly offered stacked discounts of MOP 700 + MOP 500. In the evening, benefits included a discount of around MOP 700, a buy-one-get-one offer and, for guests meeting the relevant conditions, the possibility of a private rest room.

It remains uncertain whether these campaigns can be sustained over the long term. Judged purely on current prices and inclusions, however, Majesty’s value is genuinely striking.

Majesty Spa’s Current Advantages

  • More approachable pricing in its Southeast Asian tiers
  • Substantial daytime discounts
  • A buy-one-get-one evening campaign
  • Overnight rest arrangements
  • Private rest rooms under certain conditions
  • A moderate therapist count
  • Chinese therapists currently available as well
  • Less exposure to the queue pressure of a large venue

Majesty’s main drawback is that some staff can be fairly forward in asking for tips, which can detract from the experience. Tips should be based on the service actually received and the guest’s own willingness; they should not be treated as a fixed charge.

Apart from that issue, Majesty remains a well-rounded option for price, overnight stays and its Southeast Asian roster.

If value matters more than the largest spectacle or the most extravagant decor, Majesty still deserves priority consideration.

September position: with daytime discounts, buy-one-get-one and private-rest-room benefits combined, it remains one of the market’s best-value venues.

Manhao Spa: A Low-Key Taipa Option That May Rival Shang Pin

Manhao has always been one of Macau’s more low-key new venues.

It does little promotion and attracts less online discussion than Shang Pin, so many travellers do not even realise that Taipa offers this alternative. Low profile does not mean limited ability: some venue visits in August found that Manhao’s on-site roster occasionally outperformed Shang Pin’s.

There are not many major venues currently operating in Taipa, and Manhao remains competitive on location, decor and price.

Why Manhao Deserves Attention

  • A Taipa location suited to travellers staying in the Cotai hotel district
  • Less crowding than Shang Pin experienced at its peak
  • Solid decor and room hardware
  • Competitive pricing relative to Shang Pin
  • A stronger roster at some observed points in time
  • Respectable dining, including soup

If choosing between Shang Pin and Manhao — and you have never visited Manhao — it may make sense to look at Manhao first at this stage.

Its main problem is still a lack of market visibility and the absence of a clear selling point that attracts guests and therapists. If it finds a clear position, Manhao still has room to grow in Taipa.

September position: a low-key, practical Taipa venue with less traffic pressure; first-time visitors may want to try it before looking elsewhere.

East Castle Spa (Golden Castle): Theme Rooms Remain Irreplaceable

East Castle had been sending fairly opaque signals to the market. Outsiders once thought the venue was preparing to advance a major new project, only to learn later that the principal changes were tied to M CLUB ceasing operations and the consolidation of staff.

After M CLUB closed, some therapists briefly returned to Golden Castle, lifting on-site numbers for a short period. By now, however, the overall roster has fallen back to a relatively modest level.

The people who remain after that turnover are still performing reasonably well overall.

East Castle’s greatest value today is not its therapist count. It remains one of the few Macau venues to preserve a complete set of scripted and scenario-based theme rooms.

Why Golden Castle Is Still Worth Visiting

Theme rooms remain one of Macau’s signature experiences, and East Castle is currently the most direct choice in this category.

The venue offers:

  • An aircraft cabin
  • A hospital and consultation room
  • An office
  • A classroom
  • A prison and interrogation room
  • A gym and boxing ring
  • A golf course
  • A car-wash room
  • A restaurant and other scenario spaces

The venue currently maintains a low promotional profile, but the theme rooms themselves remain distinctive.

Bringing cash is advisable. Using a credit card could previously attract a surcharge of around MOP 300; East Castle’s current card policy should still be reconfirmed before paying.

September position: the roster is not large, but East Castle remains Macau’s representative scripted-theme venue and the place to look for distinctive scenarios.

Number Nine Spa: A Good Viewing Experience, but No Clear Japan/Korea Edge

Number Nine Spa has consistently promoted its Japanese and Korean roster. In actual on-site observations, however, the numbers have been only around a “4 + 3” split, making it difficult to call this an outright defining feature for now.

Most Macau venues currently operating normally already have at least some Japanese/Korean, Caucasian, Chinese and Southeast Asian therapists. For Number Nine to create a genuine point of difference, it will need a more recognisable new draw.

Its line-up viewing environment does deserve credit.

What Makes Number Nine’s Line-up Viewing Better?

  • A comfortable viewing distance
  • Comfortable lighting levels
  • No need to view from too far away
  • A therapist count that is not overwhelming
  • Good overall visibility
  • A comparatively comfortable selection process

Apart from the close-range corridor line-up once offered by Familia Nobre, Number Nine now has one of the more distinctive viewing formats.

Its biggest pressure comes from its location near NAPE, where the number of competing venues has recently continued to rise. New openings such as Yu Sauna and Empire are all competing for the same guests, so Number Nine needs a more stable roster and clearer positioning to maintain its market presence.

September position: its Japanese/Korean distinction has yet to open a clear gap, but the lighting and viewing distance remain positive points.

Victoria Sauna: Reopened Under a New Team, Best Watched for Now

Victoria Sauna has only just resumed operations. The observation window is still too short, making it premature to draw conclusions.

Whether the new team can preserve Victoria’s previous strengths in Southeast Asian therapists, waterbed service flow and value positioning will require more reports from actual visits.

The main points to watch next are:

  • The actual on-site headcount
  • The quality of the Southeast Asian roster
  • The new team’s service processes
  • Whether prices and promotions remain stable
  • Whether distinctive campaigns return
  • Whether room arrangements run smoothly at peak times

September position: newly reopened and best placed on the watchlist until an on-site visit allows a fuller assessment.

The Excellent Sauna: Early-Shift Edge Fades, but Still Practical in the Afternoon

The Excellent Sauna was previously distinguished by therapists starting work earlier in the day.

With Yu Sauna now also able to field therapists early, that former exclusive advantage has weakened.

Current on-site observations put the roster at around 20, mainly Southeast Asian and Chinese therapists with a small Japanese/Korean contingent. Caucasian therapists may not be visible during some periods, but that does not mean there are none; it still depends on the day’s schedule.

What Advantages Does The Excellent Still Have?

  • Therapists are already on duty from noon into the afternoon
  • Practical for travellers who need to schedule an early visit
  • A boutique, smaller-venue format with less pressure when choosing
  • Comparatively proactive service from managers
  • Theme rooms and waterbeds remain available
  • No need to wait until evening for a basic roster

The Excellent and Yu Sauna are now the principal choices for earlier afternoon hours. If the visit needs to be completed in the afternoon and you do not want to wait until evening, The Excellent still has practical value.

September position: its early-session advantage is no longer exclusive, but it remains convenient in the afternoon and suits time-sensitive travellers.

How to Choose for Different Needs in September 2026

At this stage, no Macau sauna can outperform every competitor across the board. Different priorities point to different choices.

For a new 100-plus venue and the largest roster, look at Yu Sauna.

If Chinese therapists, a luxurious setting and service quality matter most, Empire still has the advantage.

For a bubble bath and the convenience of staying in Cotai without crossing to the peninsula, Shang Pin remains worth considering.

For daytime discounts, buy-one-get-one and overnight value, Majesty is currently difficult to replace.

If you are in Taipa and want to avoid Shang Pin’s crowds, try Manhao first.

For Macau’s distinctive scripted theme rooms, East Castle remains the main choice.

If line-up lighting and viewing distance matter, consider Number Nine.

For an earlier afternoon start, both The Excellent and Yu Sauna can make arrangements.

Victoria has only just reopened, so it is better to watch the new team’s consistency first.

September Price and Promotion Pitfalls

Promotions Can Change at Any Time

Some venues’ MOP 700-level discounts may be only short-term campaigns. Do not treat a three-day promotion as a fixed price.

Ask for the Final Amount Payable

Do not ask only how large the discount is. Confirm directly:

“After the menu price, discount, service fee, tourism tax, room type and every add-on are calculated, what is the final amount I need to pay?”

Buy One, Get One Does Not Mean Any Two Choices

Most buy-one-get-one offers apply only to designated tiers or times, or require a top-up. The second choice may not be freely selectable.

Requesting a Specific Therapist for a Complimentary Treatment May Cost Extra

Complimentary add-on treatments are generally assigned by the venue. Requesting a particular member of staff may cost extra.

Expect Queues at Popular Venues on Weekends

Popular venues such as Yu Sauna and Shang Pin may have queues or room waits on weekend evenings. Arriving earlier can reduce the wait.

Allow New Venues Time to Settle Their Processes

Yu Sauna, the reopened Victoria and other venues under new teams may experience short-term inconsistencies in reception, room arrangements and service duration.

Macau Sauna Market Summary — September 2026

Macau’s sauna market has officially entered another round of intense competition in September 2026.

Yu Sauna made a forceful entry with a 100-plus roster, bringing back the impact of a large-scale venue. Empire is defending its advantages in Chinese therapists and an upscale setting. Shang Pin is seeking a new balance after falling from its peak. Majesty continues to raise the value proposition through promotions and overnight capability. Manhao has emerged as Taipa’s low-key alternative. East Castle preserves its distinctiveness through scripted rooms. Number Nine’s viewing environment still stands out, while Victoria has rejoined the contest under a new team.

There is still no absolute market leader.

Macau’s sauna market is now a new horse race, with each venue competing for traffic through prices, benefits, therapists, decor and distinctive experiences. Becoming the next Number One Sauna or Oceanic Royal Spa will depend on more than one or two months of headcount: rosters must remain stable, service must keep pace and guest traffic must be retained over the long term.

In one sentence: choose Yu Sauna for the largest September roster, Empire for Chinese therapists and an upscale setting, Majesty for value and overnight stays, Shang Pin or Manhao when staying in Taipa, East Castle for scripted themes, Number Nine for line-up viewing, and The Excellent for an afternoon visit.
